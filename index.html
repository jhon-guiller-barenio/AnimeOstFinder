TAKE YOUR SOUNDTRACK WITH YOU

SakuraSound, one tap away.

Install it on your home screen for a clean, app-like listening experience. Your account, catalog, and player stay connected to the live site.

◉

Android

Open this site in Chrome, then tap Install app. If the prompt doesn’t appear, use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iOS

iPhone & iPad

Open SakuraSound in Safari, tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

SakuraSound needs an internet connection to search and play music. iPhone and iPad users install it from Safari; no App Store download is needed.
